Welcome to Red Chess. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Red Chess does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information. We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information.

Like most websites, we and our third-party partners may collect certain non-personal information automatically, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Referring website or search engine
  • General geographic location (country/region only)

2. Cookies & Advertising

Red Chess uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. For more information, see how Google uses data when you use partners' sites.

3. How We Use Information

Any automatically collected information is used solely to:

  • Understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it
  • Display relevant advertisements via Google AdSense
  • Maintain the security and performance of the site

We do not sell, trade, or share any information with third parties beyond what is described in this policy.

4. Third-Party Services

Red Chess may interact with the following services at your request:

  • Lichess – game data is fetched via the public Lichess API. We do not store this data.
  • Chess.com – game data is fetched via the Chess.com API. We do not store this data.
  • Stockfish – chess analysis runs locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server.

5. Children's Privacy

Red Chess does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: redchess@example.com
