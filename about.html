What is Red Chess?

Red Chess is a free, browser-based chess platform built for players of all skill levels. Whether you want to sharpen your game against a powerful engine or dive deep into your past games, Red Chess gives you the tools to grow as a player — beautifully and without any sign-up required.

Every feature is designed to feel fast, clean, and focused. No clutter. Just chess.

Features

♟

Play vs Bot

Challenge Stockfish — the world's strongest chess engine — at any skill level using the ELO slider.

⊹

Game Analysis

Import games from Lichess or Chess.com and get detailed move-by-move feedback and accuracy stats.

♜

No Account Needed

Jump straight in. No sign-up, no login, no personal data collected.

♛

Works in Browser

Runs entirely in your browser. Stockfish analysis happens locally — no data leaves your device.

Built With

Red Chess is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no heavy frameworks. Chess logic and analysis are powered by Stockfish, the open-source chess engine trusted by grandmasters worldwide.
